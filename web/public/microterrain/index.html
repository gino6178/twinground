Advanced settings & research methods

Actually uses high-resolution DEMs: 5 m for Moon–Earth, 20 m for Mars–Earth; Earth candidates are USGS 3DEP bare earth.

The small model has 30,024 parameters and 32-dimensional vectors and has completed self-supervised training; there is currently no validation of Moon/Mars–Earth analog accuracy. Both rankings use the same high-resolution Earth candidates.

True-relief mode adds candidates with similar relief and compares RMSE in meters after removing the median height; normalized shape keeps the original re-ranking. Neither implies the geology is the same.

Moon queries by default avoid candidates with "state map ≥80% alluvium and NLCD ≥80% shrubland, bare ground ≤5%"; Mars defaults to geometry only, keeping potentially relevant alluvial landforms. This filter cannot prove the remaining windows are rock.

Moon LROC NAC area box selection

Each of the six LROC NAC stereo elevation products has one research anchor. The area basemap shows relative elevation and hillshade within the full bounding extent of the original DTM; transparent areas lack data. It is not a lunar surface photo or material map; square dots are the centers of 3 km crops with a built 5 m DEM. Click a square dot, or click directly on an area with data to try placing a 200/500/1000 m box; saved DEMs are used first, other locations are sampled from the original DTM, and a box passes only if it is entirely valid.

Square dots correspond to existing 3 km research crops, some of which overlap; between dots, the original DTM can be sampled on demand. The area basemap is only a downsampled preview; search re-checks the native DEM, and boxes without complete valid elevation are rejected. The 5 m grid only indicates the comparison sampling spacing and does not mean lunar surface material has been determined.

Mars HiRISE/CTX area box selection

The global map locates one HiRISE and two CTX stereo elevation sources. The area basemap shows relative elevation and hillshade within the full bounding extent of the original DTM; transparent areas lack data. It is not an optical photo or material map; square dots are the centers of 3 km crops with a built 4 m HiRISE or 20 m CTX comparison DEM. Click a square dot, or click directly on an area with data to try placing a 200/500/1000 m box; HiRISE boxes can only be placed within the saved 3 km crops; CTX can be sampled from the original DTM outside the saved DEMs; a box passes only if it is entirely valid.

Square dots correspond to existing 3 km research crops; the area basemap is a downsampled preview, not a precise polygon of searchable centers. The Jezero HiRISE 3 km crop has missing cells at its edges; box selection rejects any pixel lacking data. Between CTX dots, the original DTM can be sampled.
