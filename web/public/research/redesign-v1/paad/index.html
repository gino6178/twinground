Mexico · 32.0° latitude, -114.3° longitude

Sand source

Ka'u Desert

USA · 19.353° latitude, -155.3238° longitude

Sand characteristics
High amount of volcanic glass fragments, lesser amounts of feldspar, olivine, and pyroxene (a).

Evidence and source fields
Sand source
Keanakako'i Formation is the source of all basaltic material. Sands were derived from airfall ashes from phreatomagmatic eruptions of Kilauea and reworked lava (a).
Climate
Equatorial, fully humid.
